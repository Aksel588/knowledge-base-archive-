--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATIONS: SVD & SPECTRAL THEORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1] "The approximation of one matrix by another of lower rank"
    Authors: Carl Eckart, Gale Young (1936)
    Journal: Psychometrika, 1(3), 211-218
    Link: https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02288367
    Core Concepts: The foundational proof of optimal low-rank matrix approximation
    (the Eckart-Young Theorem) using SVD.

[2] "Symmetric gauge functions and unitarily invariant norms"
    Author: Leon Mirsky (1960)
    Journal: The Quarterly Journal of Mathematics, 11(1), 50-59
    Link: https://doi.org/10.1093/qmath/11.1.50
    Core Concepts: Generalization of the Eckart-Young theorem to all unitarily 
    invariant norms (spectral/operator norm, Frobenius norm, Schatten p-norms).

[3] "Calculating the singular values and pseudo-inverse of a matrix"
    Authors: Gene Golub, William Kahan (1965)
    Journal: SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis
    Link: https://doi.org/10.1137/0702016
    Core Concepts: Numerical computation and bidiagonal reduction algorithms for SVD.

[4] MIT 18.06: Linear Algebra & SVD Lectures
    Instructor: Prof. Gilbert Strang
    Link: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/
    Notes: Fundamental geometry of the four fundamental subspaces and SVD.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. RANDOMIZED SVD & LARGE-SCALE NUMERICAL LINEAR ALGEBRA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[5] "Finding structure with randomness: Probabilistic algorithms for constructing approximate matrix decompositions"
    Authors: Nathan Halko, Per-Gunnar Martinsson, Joel A. Tropp (2011)
    arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.4061
    Journal: SIAM Review, 53(2), 217-288
    Link: https://doi.org/10.1137/090771806
    Core Concepts: Randomized SVD, low-rank matrix projections for massive matrices 
    with theoretical error bounds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PRINCIPAL COMPONENT ANALYSIS (PCA) & DATA DECOMPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[6] "On lines and planes of closest fit to systems of points in space"
    Author: Karl Pearson (1901)
    Journal: Philosophical Magazine, 2(11), 559-572
    Link: https://doi.org/10.1080/14786440109462720
    Core Concepts: Geometric derivation of best-fitting hyperplanes and variance 
    maximization.

[7] "A Tutorial on Principal Component Analysis"
    Author: Jonathon Shlens (2014)
    arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.1100
    Core Concepts: Step-by-step mathematical guide connecting covariance matrices, 
    eigenvectors, and SVD.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INTRINSIC DIMENSIONALITY & SUBSPACE DYNAMICS IN NEURAL NETWORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[8] "Measuring the Intrinsic Dimension of Objective Landscapes"
    Authors: Chunyuan Li, Heerad Farkhoor, Rosanne Liu, Jason Yosinski (2018)
    arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.08838
    Conference: ICLR 2018
    Core Concepts: Demonstrates that deep neural networks can be trained within 
    random low-dimensional subspaces.

[9] "Intrinsic Dimensionality Explains the Effectiveness of Language Model Fine-Tuning"
    Authors: Armen Aghajanyan, Luke Zettlemoyer, Sonal Gupta (2020)
    arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.13255
    Conference: ACL 2021
    Core Concepts: Proves empirically that pretraining compresses the intrinsic 
    dimension of downstream task optimization.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PARAMETER-EFFICIENT FINE-TUNING (PEFT) & LORA MECHANICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[10] "LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models"
     Authors: Edward J. Hu, Yelong Shen, Phillip Wallis, Zeyuan Allen-Zhu, Yuanzhi Li, Shean Wang, Lu Wang, Weizhu Chen (2021)
     arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685
     Conference: ICLR 2022
     Code/Repository: https://github.com/microsoft/LoRA
     Core Concepts: Weight update factorization Delta W = B * A, rank r selection, 
     zero-inference latency folding, memory savings.

[11] "QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs"
     Authors: Tim Dettmers, Artidoro Pagnoni, Ari Holtzman, Luke Zettlemoyer (2023)
     arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314
     Conference: NeurIPS 2023
     Code/Repository: https://github.com/artidoro/qlora
     Core Concepts: 4-bit NormalFloat (NF4), Double Quantization, Paged Optimizers 
     with LoRA adapters.

[12] "Scaling Down to Scale Up: A Guide to Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning"
     Authors: Vladislav Lialin, Vijeta Deshpande, Anna Rumshisky (2023)
     arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15647
     Core Concepts: Comprehensive taxonomy of adapter methods, prefix-tuning, and 
     low-rank adaptations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. SVD-DRIVEN EXTENSIONS TO LORA (ADVANCED TOPICS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[13] "PiSSA: Principal Singular Values and Singular Vectors Adaptation of Large Language Models"
     Authors: Fanxu Meng, Zhaopeng Tu, Jiarui Fang, Yuhang Wang, Zewei Sun, Liang Ding (2024)
     arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02905
     Conference: NeurIPS 2024
     Core Concepts: Uses SVD directly on W_0 to initialize the LoRA matrices with the 
     principal singular components instead of random initialization.

[14] "AdaLoRA: Adaptive Budget Allocation for Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning"
     Authors: Qingru Zhang, Minshuo Chen, Alexander Bukharin, Pengcheng He, Yu Cheng, Weizhu Chen, Tuo Zhao (2023)
     arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10512
     Conference: ICLR 2023
     Core Concepts: Dynamically adjusts rank r per layer using SVD-inspired 
     importance scoring.

[15] "SVFT: Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning with Singular Vectors"
     Authors: Vijay Lingam, Atula Tejaswi, et al. (2024)
     arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19597
     Core Concepts: Learning scaling coefficients directly over the singular vectors 
     of pretrained weight matrices.
